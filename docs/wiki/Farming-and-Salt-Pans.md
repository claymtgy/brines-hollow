# Farming & Salt Pans

1. Use a **hoe** on soil to till a plot.
2. Use seeds on the tilled plot to plant them.
3. Sweet-soil crops need fresh water (rain helps). Brackish crops like samphire want salty tidal mud.
4. Harvest when they're ripe. Some crops, like samphire, grow back after harvest.

**Salinity** matters: soil near the sea is saltier, and each crop grows in its own range.
See the [crops table](generated/crops.md).

## Salt pans
Place a **Clay Salt Pan** between the tide lines. High tide floods it with seawater. As the
tide goes out it dries in the sun and leaves **Sea Salt**, which you use for preserving and brining.
