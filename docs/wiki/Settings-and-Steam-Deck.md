# Settings & Steam Deck

Open **Esc → Settings**. It has five tabs:

- **Controls:** first-person camera, mouse sensitivity, invert look, controller look speed,
  field of view, camera distance and side offset, shoulder, fading leaves in front of you.
- **Graphics:** a quality preset, render scale (0 = auto), upscaler, anti-aliasing, shadows,
  view distance, glow, fog, window mode, V-Sync and a frame cap.
- **Interface:** UI scale.
- **Keys:** rebind any action. Click a button, then press the new key.
- **Audio:** master, ambience (sea, wind, rain) and effects volume.

## Steam Deck and gamepads
- Xbox, PlayStation (DualShock / DualSense) and Steam Deck controls all work, and the on-screen
  prompts show the right buttons for whichever you're using.
- On the Deck, lower the preset or render scale for battery life.
- The back paddles are mapped to **dodge** (paddle 1) and **sneak** (paddle 2), on top of the
  usual buttons. See [Controls](Controls.md).
