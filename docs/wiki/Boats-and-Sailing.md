# Boats & Sailing

There's a rowboat tied at the pier. You can build more with the **Rowboat** blueprint.

- **Board:** walk up to the boat and press F.
- **Row:** W / S forward and back, A / D turn. Hold Shift to pull hard (uses stamina).
- **Step off:** press F. Near land you step ashore. Out on open water you go **over the side
  and swim**, so you can dive for things or fish from the water. Swim back and press F to climb in.
- Storms bring big swell. Keep the bow into the waves.
- Boats drift with the current while nobody's rowing, so tie up at a pier.

Out past the shallows you'll find deep-sea fish, seals and puffins on the skerries, and the
occasional whale passing by (see [Creatures & Wildlife](Creatures-and-Wildlife.md)).
