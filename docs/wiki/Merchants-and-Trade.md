# Merchants & Trade

- A merchant ship docks at the pier from about **08:40 to 14:50** in-game. Visits alternate
  between the **Raft Peddler** and a bigger **galleon**.
- Talk to the merchant (F) to buy and sell. The currency is **Brass Guilders**.
- They sell goods, tools and **recipe scrolls**, and they buy your fish, salt and crafts.
- A merchant's stock (including what you've already bought out) and the count of visits are
  saved with the world, so saving and reloading won't reroll their wares.
