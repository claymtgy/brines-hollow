# Crafting & Recipes

- **Hand crafting:** open your pack (I / Tab) to make simple tools, planks, campfires and stations.
- **Stations:** walk up to a workbench, campfire, hearth pot, smokehouse, saltery, teahouse,
  furnace or anvil and press F. Some stations need fuel, and some recipes take time.

## Learning recipes
Most recipes are known from the start. You learn the rest:
- **by discovery:** the first time a key ingredient lands in your pack (your first salmon
  teaches smoked salmon strips and the smokehouse);
- **from Wren's lessons** (see [Getting Started](Getting-Started.md));
- **from villagers:** each drifter you recruit teaches you their trade's recipes;
- **from recipe scrolls:** merchants sell some, and reading one adds it to your book;
- **from legendary catches:** landing a legendary fish teaches you its secret dish (the
  **Abyssal Ray** teaches *Leviathan Feast*, the **Ghost Squid** *Phosphor Broth*, the
  **Lightning Eel** *Storm-Smoked Eel*).

## The Recipe Book
Open the quest log (J) and pick the **Recipe Book** tab. It shows how many recipes you
know, grouped by station. Pick a station to see each known recipe with its ingredients.
Recipes you haven't learned show as **???** with a hint about where to learn them.

**Copying scrolls:** next to a recipe you know, press **Copy to scroll** (costs 2 Shore
Wheat) to write it onto a scroll you can give or trade to another player. If you pick up a
scroll you already know, you keep it instead of reading it.

## Rare recipe scrolls
Some scrolls are only found out in the world: in every **Wreck Locker** and **Sunken Cache**,
and now and then in a cave chest or a Mud-Claw hoard. A scroll is read the moment you pick it
up. Each world deals out its own shuffled set, so explore widely to find them all. Two
recipes can *only* be learned this way:
- **Fine sea-brass** (from the strongbox in each old brass mine);
- **the stone well** (found in wrecks, sumps and caves).

Others among them (bouillabaisse, chicory coffee, fish rations, the storage crate, the salt
pan, the barber's mirror) can also come from drifters, quests or merchants.

The recipes you know belong to your character in that world and are saved with you. The
[recipe table](generated/recipes.md) lists every recipe and how each locked one is unlocked.
