# Island Villages

In the big archipelago (worlds made since the islands grew), two or three of the larger
islands have a small fishing village. Classic-size worlds don't have them.

## Finding one
Each village sits on a flat stretch near its island's coast, with a pier running out into the
sea. Look for red roofs, lanterns and a field of wheat behind the cottages. When you walk into a
village you see its name (e.g. *Skua Harbour on Skua Holm*), and it's marked on your sea chart
with a small blue hut. Each character keeps their own list of villages they've found.

## The villagers
Every village has five residents, each with a name and a job:
- **Trader**: sells the island's goods and buys your catch and crops (see below).
- **Village Elder**: has a run of requests for you (see below).
- **Fisher**: works at the end of the pier.
- **Farmer**: tends the field behind the cottages.
- **Net-Mender**: works by the drying rack.

They head out to work after sunrise, potter about their spot through the day, and walk home
in the evening. **At night they're indoors**, so come back in the morning if you want to
talk or trade. Walk up to anyone and press **F** to talk.

## The trader
Each village trader keeps their own stock, shaped by their island:
- meadow and hill villages: seeds, saplings and timber;
- atoll villages: samphire and tide-kale seed, smoked fish;
- crag and mesa villages: copper ore and iron ingots (crags sometimes have a sea-brass ingot).

They all buy the same goods as the visiting merchant ships (fish, crops, salt, driftwood, stone,
chitin). Trades work the same way too (see [Merchants & Trade](Merchants-and-Trade.md)), but
village traders don't take export manifests. A trader's stock is shared by everyone in the
world, it doesn't restock, and it's saved with the world.

## The elder's requests
The elder (marked **!**) asks for a load of something the village is short of: salt, planks,
mackerel, smoked fish, copper ore, wheat, sea glass, driftwood, kelp or stone. Each elder has a
run of **five** requests in their own order. Accepted requests appear in your quest log. Once
you're carrying the goods, the marker turns to **?**: talk to the elder and hand them over for
brass. After all five, the village counts you as a friend.

Requests are per player: in co-op, each of you has your own run with each elder.
