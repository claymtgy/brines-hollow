# Co-op & Dedicated Servers

- **Host:** from the main menu, host a world. Friends join with your IP address. The default
  port is **24565** (UDP). Forward it on your router if friends are joining over the internet.
- **Join:** enter the host's address. Your character in that world is kept on the host's save,
  so you pick up where you left off next time you join.
- The host runs the world: time, tides, weather, creatures, raids and merchants. Fast-forwarding
  time (hold T) only works for the host.
- **Dedicated server:** run `godot --headless --path . -- --server` (or `--server=PORT`) to keep a world up
  without anyone playing on that machine.

Livestock move live for everyone. Joined players can lead, pet, feed, milk and shear
animals just like the host. A befriended otter remembers its friend across sessions.
