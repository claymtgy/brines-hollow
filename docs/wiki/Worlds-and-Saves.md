# Worlds & Saves

- The main menu lists your worlds. Pick one to play, or **+ Create New World** to start a new one.
- **Every world has its own characters.** Your inventory, health, known recipes and bed
  spot belong to that world. Nothing carries over between worlds.
- **Delete World:** select a world, press **Delete World**, then press it again to confirm.
  The world is moved to the **Recycle Bin**, so you can still restore it from there.
- The game saves on its own as you play, and when you quit or return to the menu.
- Saves live in `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Brine's Hollow\worlds\<world name>\`.
