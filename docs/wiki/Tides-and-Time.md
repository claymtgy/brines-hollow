# Tides & Time

- A full day lasts **12 real minutes**: sunrise a quarter of the way through, noon at half, sunset at three quarters.
- The sea rises and falls about **1.26 m** either side of mid-tide, **twice a day**. The flats
  between the tide lines (wet sand) are where crab pots, salt pans and tide-crabs live.
- Playing solo or as host, **hold T** to fast-forward and watch the tide move.
- Sleeping in a Fisher's Bunk at night skips to dawn for everyone. It also sets where you wake
  if you faint, and that spot is saved with your character.

## What the tide changes
| Low tide | High tide |
|---|---|
| Tidal pools: flounder bite | Mackerel and salmon come in close |
| Tide-crabs scuttle on the flats | Crab pots fish (bait them first) |
| Seals haul out on the beach | Salt pans flood, then dry in the sun as the tide goes out |
| The sea-cave mouth opens | Cave entrances go under |
