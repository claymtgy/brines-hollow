# Village & Drifters

From time to time a **drifter** washes up on the beach. Talk to them (F) to see what they need
before they'll stay. It's usually a lit, roofed room with a bed, and sometimes their trade's station.

- Once they move in, a drifter works their trade, gives you goods now and then, and **teaches
  you their trade's recipes** (see [Crafting](Crafting-and-Recipes.md)).
- Their room counts as a home while it's enclosed, lit and has a bed. Build huts with the
  blueprints and light them with lanterns.
- The more drifters stay, the busier the village gets, and merchants pay more attention to you.

## Castaways
Three castaways are trapped in shipwrecks on the outer islands: Ansel the carpenter, Petra
the fisher and Noll the trader. They're pinned under the timbers, so you can only reach them
at **low tide**. Talk to one to free them, and they sail home to your village on their own.
After that they're drifters like any other.

## Founding your village: the Tide Hearth
Build a **Tide Hearth** (16 cobblestone, 8 driftwood, 2 sea-brass ingots). The first one you
place **founds your village** there. Until you have a hearth your settlement stays a camp,
however many drifters you have.

- The hearth is the village's cooking fire. Villagers eat there.
- Press F at the hearth to **collect the town depot** (the goods your villagers have made)
  and hear how the town is doing.

## Morale
How happy your villagers are depends on:
- **Homes:** a drifter without a valid room (enclosed, lit, with a bed) is unhappy. Better
  rooms make them happier.
- **Food variety:** the more different foods in the depot and pantry barrels, the better.
- **Safety:** lanterns around town help, and a **Harbour Bell** helps a lot.
- **The hearth** itself.

Morale moves slowly toward where these put it. The hearth tells you what's holding it back.
