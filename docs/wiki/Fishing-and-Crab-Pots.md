# Fishing & Crab Pots

## Rod fishing
1. Equip a fishing rod and face the water.
2. **Hold** use (LMB / RT) to charge the cast, then let go. The further you charge, the
   further the lure flies. A red-and-white float lands and sits on the water where you can see it.
   If you aim at land, the lure drops at the furthest water along that line.
3. Wait for a bite: the float bobs and dips. Press use to strike.
4. Reel by holding use. Ease off when the line goes tight or it'll snap.

**Which fish bite** depends on where you are (open sea, shallows, tidal pools, river mouth,
sea caves), the time of day, the tide and the weather. See the [fish table](generated/fish.md).

## Crab pots
Set a crab pot on the wet sand between the tide lines, or off the pier. **Bait** it (a kelp frond,
mackerel, flounder or a shore crab), and while the tide covers it, it may catch shore crabs
or oysters. Collect the catch with F when the tide is out.

## Cooking
Cook fish at a campfire or a hearth pot, smoke them in a smokehouse, or salt and brine them so
they keep. See [Food & Vitality](Food-and-Vitality.md).
