# Creatures & Wildlife

Base health and other numbers for every creature: [creatures table](generated/creatures.md).

## Ambient wildlife (harmless)
None of these can be hurt. Hitting one just startles it.

| Animal | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal hare | grassy dunes and clifftops | bolts if you run at it; sneak (Ctrl) to get close |
| Harbour seal | hauls out on beaches at low tide, swims at high | slips into the sea if you crowd it |
| Puffin | sea cliffs and skerries | flies off if startled |
| Sea otter | kelp beds and calm shallows | **befriend** it: hold a fish, stand still beside it and press F; a friendly otter now and then brings you a gift, and it finds you again after a reload or when you rejoin |
| Grey heron | tidal pools and the river mouth | stalks the shallows; flaps off if you get close |
| Migrating whale | far out at sea | passes by now and then; watch for its spout |

Animals leave **tracks** in sand and mud, which is how you can tell where a fox has been.

## Hostile creatures
Cave eels, crabs and the like. See the [creatures table](generated/creatures.md). Cave eels
**won't come out in light**: a lantern post or a lit station (campfire, smokehouse, furnace,
hearth pot) keeps the area around it clear.

## Gloom-Crawlers
Pale, blind cave hunters that cling to the ceilings of **deep caverns and mines**. They
hunt by sound: walk or run beneath one and it drops on you from above with a hiss as it
lets go. Listen for the hiss and step aside.

- **Sneak (Ctrl) under them** and they don't hear you.
- **Light drives them off.** They won't come within a few metres of a lantern or a lit
  station, so torch your route through a cavern.
- On the ground they bite and keep hunting by sound, but they give up after a few seconds
  and climb back to the roof.
- They drop **gloom glands** (they glow) and chitin.

## Sea-Reaver outposts
Marooned pirates hold stockades on the **far islands**, well away from home: palisade walls,
gate towers with lanterns, a watchtower, tents, and a **Reaver Strongbox** with brass, iron,
salvage and a rare recipe scroll. When you first come near one it's marked on your chart.

- **Cutlass reavers** close in with a two-cut combo. **Harpooners** throw from range, and
  one always keeps the watchtower.
- Reavers **see** rather than hear. Stay out of sight, and at night don't carry a torch: it
  gives you away.
- The **War Horn Totem** stands inside the gate. When the reavers spot you it sounds and two
  more come running. Wipe out the garrison and a new one arrives five minutes later, as long
  as the totem stands.
- **Topple the totem** (F) once no reaver is within about 30 m and the outpost is **captured
  for good**: no more reavers, and a bed appears in the tents so you can use it as a forward
  base.
- Garrisons: **3 + 1 harpooner on Cozy (they never attack), 4 + 2 on Tidewatch, 5 + 3 on
  Undertow**.

## The Barnacled Hulk
On the tidal flats of a far island, beneath two tall stone outcrops, something huge lies
buried in the sand. Come close and **the Barnacled Hulk** rises: a colossal hermit crab in a
barnacle-crusted shell.

- **Its shell turns most blows.** Hitting it head-on barely scratches it until the shell is
  cracked.
- **Drop the boulders.** Loose boulders sit on top of the outcrops. Climb up and push one (F)
  when the Hulk is below: each hit cracks the shell and does heavy damage. They roll back
  into place after a while.
- **Use the tide.** When the tide falls below mid, the Hulk bogs down in the mud of the flats
  for a few seconds. While it's mired it can't fight back and takes much more damage.
- It sweeps with its claw and slams its shell down; both are slow, so watch and dodge.
- Beaten, it drops **The Hulk's Pearl**, heavy chitin, brass and sea glass, and it stays
  beaten for everyone in the world.

## Mud-Claw camps
Crab-folk hold driftwood camps on some of the outer islands: a palisade of posts with a
gate, three mud domes, a shell totem and a fire pit. There are never any on your home island
or within about 300 m of your hut. When you first come near one, it's marked on your sea chart.

- **Swarmers** are small and quick and come in a pack, but only two press an attack at once.
  **The brute** is slow and heavily plated, and its overhead slam has a long wind-up you can
  read and punish.
- **By day the camp dozes** in its domes and only wakes to noise. Sneak (Ctrl) and you can
  reach the camp chest. Running or fighting wakes the lot.
- **Light keeps swarmers off.** Carry a lantern and they'll circle and click but won't close.
  The brute doesn't care.
- **They won't leave home.** Get well clear of the palisade, or take to the water, and they
  give up and go back.
- Beat the whole garrison and the camp is **cleared for good**, for everyone in the world.
  Its mark on the chart is crossed out. They drop **heavy chitin plate**.
- How many guard a camp: **3 swarmers + a brute on Cozy, 4 + 1 on Tidewatch, 6 + 1 on
  Undertow**. On Cozy they never attack: they scuttle into their domes when you come close.

## Raiders: foxes and stoats
At night a **red fox** or a **stoat** may come for your pen. Stoats only steal eggs.
How many raids a night: **1 on Cozy, 2 on Tidewatch, 3 on Undertow**.

What a raider takes:
- **Cozy:** an egg or some feed. Never an animal.
- **Tidewatch:** an egg or feed, sometimes a duck.
- **Undertow:** usually a duck.

A carried duck is **dropped if you chase the raider down** before it reaches its den.

### How to stop them
| Defence | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Lantern post** or a lit station near the pen | raiders won't go into the light |
| **Pen Fence** all round, with a **Latched Pen Gate** | they can't get in. The rail fence has a gap they slip under |
| **Chase them** | run at a raider and it bolts, dropping anything it carried |
| **Live trap** | bait it; a raider within about 12 blocks goes for the bait and gets caught. Use **release** to let it go far away, and it won't be back to this pen for a while |
| **Fill the den** | follow the tracks to the den and fill it in (F). They have to dig a new one further off |
| **Guard dog** (Guard Dog Kennel) | the dog sees off any fox, stoat or shore rat within 16 m, barking so everyone hears |

A pen that sees off a raider is remembered, and raiders are slower to try it again.

## Shore rats
On the **nights after a storm**, shore rats come up off the tideline after your food. They go
for **open chests and crates with food in them** (fish, crops, meals) and eat a bite or two:
1 on Cozy, 2 on Tidewatch, 3 on Undertow. They leave everything else alone. On Undertow they
sometimes come in pairs.

| Defence | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sealed Pantry Barrel** | holds any food and rats can't get in. The Watertight Sea Barrel is rat-proof too |
| **Dock cat** (Dock Cat's Basket) | chases off rats (and stoats) within 12 m |
| **Guard dog** | sees rats off within 16 m |
| **Light**, **chasing**, **live trap** | work on rats just like on foxes |

## Sea eagles
By day a **sea eagle** may circle high over a duck that's out in the open, then stoop on it.
You get a warning while it circles. On Cozy the stoop only scatters the ducks. On Tidewatch it
sometimes carries one off, and on Undertow it usually does.

| Defence | What it does |
|---|---|
| **A roof** | ducks with anything solid over them (a roofed pen, a coop, an overhang) are safe |
| **Oilskin Scarecrow** | eagles won't stoop within 14 m of one (gulls keep clear too) |
| **Stay with your ducks** | an eagle won't stoop with a player within 6 m of its target |
| **Hit it** | an eagle carrying a duck drops it when struck |

## The Harbour Bell
Build a **Harbour Bell** in the middle of your farm or village. It's an alarm for everyone:
- **When a raid starts** within 90 m of the bell (fox, stoat, shore rat or sea eagle), it rings
  by itself. Everyone in the world gets the message with what and where ("Fox raid 30 m
  north-east of the bell"), the toll carries across the island, and a **shaft of light marks
  the spot** for about 25 seconds.
- **Ring it by hand** (F) to call everyone to the bell. The din also sends any raider within
  25 m running. After ringing, it hums for a few seconds before it can ring again.
