# Difficulty Modes

You pick the difficulty when you create a world. Everyone in that world plays on it.

| | Cozy | Tidewatch | Undertow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage you take | none | normal | x1.6 |
| Creature health | normal | normal | x1.5 |
| Fox raids a night | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Raiders take | eggs or feed only | eggs or feed, sometimes a duck | usually a duck |
| Shore rats (nights after a storm) take | 1 food | 2 food | 3 food |
| Sea eagle stoops a day | 1, scatters only | 1, sometimes takes a duck | 2, usually takes a duck |
