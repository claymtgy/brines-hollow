# Getting Started

You wash ashore with a few rough tools. **Wren the Wayfarer**, a travelling fisher, walks up
and teaches you the basics in seven short lessons. Each one ends back with Wren, who pays you
and teaches you recipes. You can skip the tutorial when Wren first speaks to you.

| # | Lesson | What you do | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find Your Feet | Dig 6 handfuls of sand, dirt or stone with the pickaxe | 8 driftwood |
| 2 | Make Your Mark | Place 5 blocks with the hammer (B opens the build menu) | 12 planks, Storage Crate recipe |
| 3 | Tools of the Trade | Craft anything, in your pack or at a workbench | a crab pot, 4 kelp, Crab Pot recipe |
| 4 | Let the Tide Work | Set the crab pot on the wet sand between the tide lines | a salt pan, Salt Pan recipe |
| 5 | A Line in the Water | Catch a fish | 2 mackerel, Roasted Mackerel and Sea Barrel recipes |
| 6 | A Warm Belly | Cook a fish at a campfire and eat it (right button) | Smokehouse and Hearth Pot recipes |
| 7 | Beyond the Shallows | Row the boat at the pier out onto the water | 30 Brass Guilders, Shore Chart recipe |

## Your first day, in short
1. Dig and build. Everything you dig comes back as material (one item for every four blocks),
   and building costs exactly what digging gives.
2. Set a crab pot and a salt pan on the flats. The tide works them for you.
3. Fish from the pier (see [Fishing](Fishing-and-Crab-Pots.md)). Mackerel bite at high tide by day.
4. Eat before hard work. Meals raise your health and stamina (see [Food & Vitality](Food-and-Vitality.md)).
5. Before your first night, put a lantern post by the duck pen (see [Livestock & Pens](Livestock-and-Pens.md)).
