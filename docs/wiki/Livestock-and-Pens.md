# Livestock & Pens

You start with a couple of ducks near the landing. Goats and sheep come later.

- **Ducks** lay eggs. **Goats** give milk. **Sheep** give wool.
- Animals need a **Feed Trough** with feed in it, and water. A **Duck Coop** gives ducks
  somewhere to roost and lay.
- Hold a feed item and an animal will follow you, which is how you lead it into a pen.

## Building a pen that holds
| Fence | Ducks | Goats / sheep | Foxes & stoats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rail **Fence** | walk out under it | held | get in under it |
| **Pen Fence** (boarded) | held | held | kept out |
| **Latched Pen Gate**, shut | held | held | kept out |
| Latched Pen Gate, open | walk out | walk out | walk in |

Animals step up one-block ledges and walk around placed furniture. They can't climb walls.

## Keeping raiders off
Foxes and stoats come for the pen at night, and sea eagles by day. See
[Creatures & Wildlife](Creatures-and-Wildlife.md#raiders-foxes-and-stoats) for every way to stop
them. The short version: **a roofed pen with a Pen Fence all round, a lantern post, and a guard
dog's kennel nearby.** A scarecrow covers an open pen against eagles.

In co-op, everyone sees the animals move, and anyone holding wheat or kale (or the whistle)
can lead them.
