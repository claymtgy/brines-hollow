# FAQ

**My ducks keep getting out.**
A rail Fence has a gap under the bottom rail. Use **Pen Fence** and a **Latched Pen Gate**,
and keep the gate shut. See [Livestock & Pens](Livestock-and-Pens.md).

**Something keeps taking my eggs at night.**
A fox. Put a lantern post by the pen, and see [Creatures & Wildlife](Creatures-and-Wildlife.md#how-to-stop-them).

**Food keeps vanishing from my chest after a storm.**
Shore rats. Keep food in a Sealed Pantry Barrel, or put a dock cat's basket by your stores.

**A big bird took one of my ducks in broad daylight.**
A sea eagle. Roof the pen, or put up a scarecrow. Hit the eagle to make it drop the duck.

**How do I know a raid is happening when I'm across the island?**
Build a Harbour Bell. It rings for everyone and lights a beacon over the raid.

**Where are the other islands?**
Take the rowboat out from the pier. There are five larger islands and many islets around
Brine's Hollow. Each one is named and added to your chart the first time you land on it.

**My cast says "No water there".**
Face open water deep enough for a float. If you aim at land, the lure drops at the furthest
water along the line. If there isn't any, try again closer to the edge.

**Can I get off the boat away from land?**
Yes. Press F out on the water and you go over the side and swim.

**Did my inventory follow me into a new world?**
It shouldn't, because every world keeps its own characters. If you see that, please report it.

**I deleted a world by accident.**
It's in your Recycle Bin. Restore it there and it shows up in the menu again.

**Where do I wake up if I faint?**
At the last bunk you slept in. That spot is saved with your character in that world.

**How do I see the tide change faster?**
Solo or as host, hold T.
