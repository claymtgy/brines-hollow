# Caves & Mining

The big archipelago (worlds made since the islands grew) is riddled with caves. Worlds in
the classic, smaller size keep their single sea cave on the home island.

## Tidal sea caves
Look along the cliffs. Every larger island has a sea cave bored into its cliff face, and the
home island has two. The mouth sits at sea level, so **at high tide it's under water** and at
low tide you can wade or row in. Inside, the passage climbs to a **sandy grotto** lit by
glowing moss. The high tide just reaches the grotto floor.

## Karst caverns
Under the high ground of the larger islands are caverns: two to four wide chambers joined by
passages, with stalactites hanging from the roof and stalagmites rising from the floor. Each
cavern has one passage that climbs out to the hillside. Look for a dark opening in a slope.

## Old brass mines
On a few outer islands, an abandoned **brass mine** runs into a hillside: look for a timbered
portal with a heavy lintel. A square-cut adit with old rails and lanterns leads to a domed
**gallery** held up by pit props. Its walls are crusted with pale blue-green **calamine**
and copper. A **Miner's Strongbox** in the gallery holds calamine, copper, brass, and the
scroll for **fine sea-brass**, which only the old miners knew.

Deep caverns and mines are where **Gloom-Crawlers** lurk on the ceilings. Bring light and
tread softly. See [Creatures & Wildlife](Creatures-and-Wildlife.md).

## Flooded sumps
Below the first chamber of each karst cavern, a shaft drops past the lowest tide into a
drowned passage and a flooded chamber, lit by glowing moss. It's a real dive, so go with full
stamina. At the bottom a **Sunken Cache** holds the best of the caves: sea glass, brass and
iron, sometimes heavy chitin, and a rare recipe scroll.

## Freshwater springs and wells
Each karst cavern also has a **freshwater spring** bubbling up on its floor. Press F to fill
three flasks of **spring water**, and it never runs dry. Every island village has a
**stone well** too, and you can learn to build your own (its recipe is a rare scroll). A well
holds four flasks and seeps back one about every minute and a half.

Spring water is a drink (it helps stamina recover; see [Food & Vitality](Food-and-Vitality.md)).
It's also what the furnace quenches **fine sea-brass** in: one copper ore, one calamine ore and
one flask make three sea-brass ingots.

## Barnacled chests
Each grotto and cavern holds one **Barnacled Chest** (the deepest chamber, in a cavern):
- sea-cave chests: polished sea glass, copper ore, sometimes a sea-brass ingot;
- cavern chests: sea glass, copper ore, brine-iron ore, sometimes an iron ingot;
- and now and then a rare recipe scroll in either.

They're there once per world, shared by everyone, and can't be picked up.

## Ore veins
Cave walls are streaked with ore:
- **Copper veins** (orange-brown) are common in sea caves and caverns. Any pickaxe mines them.
- **Brine-iron veins** (rusty red) are mostly in the caverns. They need a **tier-2 pickaxe**; a
  weaker one just chips the vein away with nothing to show for it.
- **Calamine** (pale blue-green crust) is only found in the old brass mines. Any pickaxe mines it.

Digging into a vein always gives ore (about one per two vein voxels), even when the rest of
your swing is plain stone. Out in
the open, plain stone only rarely turns up a nugget in the big archipelago (classic worlds
keep their old, more generous stone drops, since they have no veins). Smelt ore into ingots (by hand,
or faster at a furnace); see [Crafting & Recipes](Crafting-and-Recipes.md).
