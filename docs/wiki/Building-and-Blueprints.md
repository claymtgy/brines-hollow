# Building & Blueprints

| Tool | Slot | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Pickaxe | 1 | digs blocks; you get one item for every four blocks |
| Hammer | 2 | places blocks for one item per four blocks. **B** opens the build menu (blocks, structures, furniture) |
| Blueprints | 3 | places whole structures (walls, huts, docks, fences) and furniture |

- **Brush size** (Z / X) digs or places bigger cubes. **R** rotates a blueprint. **G** toggles
  the 6-voxel grid so walls line up.
- A voxel is 0.18 m; a person is about 10 voxels tall.
- Lantern blocks and lamp posts give real light. Drifters need a lit room, and light keeps
  [cave eels and foxes](Creatures-and-Wildlife.md) away.

## Fences for animal pens
The blueprints list has two fences, and they behave differently:
- **Fence** (rail fence): a top and a middle rail on posts. It holds goats and sheep, but
  there's a gap under the bottom rail: **ducks wander out, and foxes and stoats get in.**
- **Pen Fence**: boards right down to the ground. It holds every pen animal and keeps raiders out.
- Leave a gap for a **Latched Pen Gate** (it's in the furniture list). F opens and closes it,
  and when it's shut nothing gets through.

## Farm guardians and stores (furniture list, made at the workbench)
- **Guard Dog Kennel:** comes with a dog that sees off foxes, stoats and rats within 16 m.
- **Dock Cat's Basket:** comes with a cat that keeps rats away within 12 m.
- **Oilskin Scarecrow:** sea eagles and gulls keep clear within 14 m.
- **Sealed Pantry Barrel:** stores any food, and shore rats can't get in.
- **Harbour Bell:** the village alarm (2 iron ingots, 8 planks, 4 cobblestone). See
  [Creatures & Wildlife](Creatures-and-Wildlife.md#the-harbour-bell).
