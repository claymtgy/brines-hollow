# Brine's Hollow Player Wiki

A cozy co-op coastal voxel sandbox: wash ashore, dig and build, fish the tides, farm the salt
flats, keep a little flock, and grow a fishing village out of the drifters who wash up after you.

> in the same change that adds the feature.
